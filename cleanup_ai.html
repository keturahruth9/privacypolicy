Cleanup AI Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

Welcome to Cleanup AI. We built Cleanup AI to help you review duplicate photos, similar images, screenshots, videos, and other media on your device in a simple and private way. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

1. What Cleanup AI Collects

Cleanup AI does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. You can use the app without creating an account, signing in, or sharing your personal identity with us.

  • Your photos and videos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Your name, email address, contacts, and passwords are not collected by the app for cleanup features.
  • Your private media is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

2. Photo and Video Access

Cleanup AI requests access to your photo library so it can analyze your media and help you review duplicate items, similar items, screenshots, large videos, and other cleanup suggestions.

All scanning and analysis are designed to happen locally on your device. Your photos and videos stay on your phone unless you explicitly choose to share or export them yourself.

3. How Cleanup AI Uses Permissions

The app may request access only to the permissions needed for its core features.

  • Photo Library Access to review and organize your media.
  • Notifications only if enabled, to remind you about duplicate-related cleanup opportunities.
  • Biometric Access only if you choose to enable secure unlock features inside the app.

These permissions are used only to support app functionality and not for external tracking.

4. On-Device AI and Media Analysis

Cleanup AI uses intelligent processing to identify duplicate photos, similar images, blurry content, screenshots, videos, and other media categories.

This processing is intended to happen on-device, which means your media does not need to leave your phone for the app to generate review suggestions.

5. Local Storage

To improve your experience, Cleanup AI may store app preferences, scan state, cached grouping results, selection state, and related app data locally on your device.

This local data is used only to help the app resume scans faster, remember your choices, and provide a smoother cleanup experience.

6. Sharing and Deletion Actions

If you choose to share, archive, favorite, or delete media using Cleanup AI, those actions are triggered only by your direct interaction. Cleanup AI does not perform automatic sharing or deletion without your input.

7. Third-Party Services

Cleanup AI may use trusted third-party services that support app functionality, such as platform services for app reviews, notifications, crash reporting, or analytics. These services are not used to access your personal photo library content.

8. Children's Privacy

Cleanup AI does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app does not require account registration for its main cleanup features, personal identity information is not required to use the app.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements, feature changes, or legal requirements. If we make updates, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
