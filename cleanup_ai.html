Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Mar 1, 2026

Welcome to Cleanup AI. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Cleanup AI does not collect, store, or sell personal information such as:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Personal photos or videos from your device
  • Device identifiers or precise location data

2. Photo Library Access

Cleanup AI requires access to your device's photo library to help identify duplicate, similar, blurred, or unnecessary photos and videos.

All photo analysis and cleanup operations occur locally on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to our servers.

The app only accesses the photos necessary to provide cleanup suggestions and does not copy, store, or transmit your media externally.

3. Permissions

The app may request the following permission:

  • Photo Library Access – to scan and analyze photos for duplicates, similar images, large videos, and cleanup suggestions

This permission is required for the core functionality of the app and is only used for organizing and managing your media.

4. AI Processing

Cleanup AI uses intelligent algorithms to detect duplicate photos, blurry images, screenshots, and large media files.

This analysis happens entirely on-device, meaning your photos never leave your phone during processing.

5. Data Storage

Cleanup AI does not maintain user accounts or external databases. Any preferences or scan results remain stored locally on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or subscription management to improve the app experience. These services do not have access to your photos or personal media.

7. Children’s Privacy

Cleanup AI does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. The app can be used without creating an account or providing personal information.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
