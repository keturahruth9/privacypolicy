Welcome to Adlytics • Ad Earnings Tracker (com.vectorinfo.admob), developed and maintained by VectorInfo. This app helps you view your AdMob earnings, apps, ad units, country reports, and related account performance in one place. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Access

To provide the core features of Adlytics • Ad Earnings Tracker, VectorInfo may access certain account and reporting information after you sign in with your Google account.

  • Your Google account basic profile information, such as your name and email address.
  • Your AdMob account details, such as publisher ID, currency, and reporting time zone.
  • Your AdMob reporting data, such as earnings, impressions, clicks, requests, match rate, CTR, eCPM, apps, ad units, and country-level performance.

2. Google Sign-In and AdMob Access

Adlytics uses Google Sign-In to authenticate you and access your AdMob data only after you grant permission.

The app is designed to use your AdMob account data only to show your reports and account-related insights inside the app. It does not use your Google sign-in to post, manage ads, or make account changes on your behalf.

3. How Your Information Is Used

The information accessed by Adlytics • Ad Earnings Tracker is used only to support the app's features and improve your experience.

  • To sign you in and keep your session active.
  • To fetch and display earnings and performance reports.
  • To show app-wise, ad unit-wise, and country-wise breakdowns.
  • To generate exports such as monthly CSV revenue reports.
  • To improve loading performance through secure caching and local storage.

4. Local Storage and Caching

Adlytics may store certain data locally on your device, such as cached report data, selected filters, login state, and display preferences.

This helps the app load faster and provide a smoother experience. Local cached data is used only for app functionality and is not sold or shared for advertising.

5. Firebase and Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services such as Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore, and Google AdMob APIs to support sign-in, reporting, diagnostics, analytics, and app functionality.

These services may process limited account, device, diagnostics, or app usage information as needed to provide their services.

6. Data Sharing

VectorInfo does not sell your personal information or AdMob reporting data collected through Adlytics • Ad Earnings Tracker.

  • Your AdMob report data is not sold to third parties.
  • Your account information is not shared for advertising purposes.
  • Data may be processed by service providers only to support core app functionality.

7. Data Retention and Security

VectorInfo takes reasonable steps to protect the information used by Adlytics • Ad Earnings Tracker. Some data may be retained for as long as needed to provide the app's features, maintain login state, improve performance, or comply with legal obligations.

While we work to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using the app at any time. You can also revoke Google account access through your Google account settings if you no longer want the app to access your AdMob information.

If you remove the app from your device, locally stored app data may also be removed, subject to your device and platform behavior.

9. Children's Privacy

Adlytics • Ad Earnings Tracker is not intended for children under 13. The app is built for users who manage or review AdMob account data and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
