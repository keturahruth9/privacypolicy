CalculatorVault Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CalculatorVault handles photos, videos, permissions, local vault storage, iCloud sync through CloudKit, and advertising services.

Effective Date: March 19, 2026

Primary Storage

Vault files are stored locally on your device inside the app's private storage.

1. Information We Handle

CalculatorVault does not require account registration, username creation, or password submission to our servers.

2. Local Vault Storage

Photos and videos you add to the vault are stored locally in the app's document directory on your device. This local storage is used to display, manage, and restore your vault content inside the app.

3. CloudKit and iCloud Backup

CalculatorVault may sync photos and videos to your private iCloud CloudKit database for backup and restore.

4. Permissions We Request

  • Camera - to capture photos for the vault
  • Photo Library - to import photos and videos into the vault
  • Photo Library Add Access - when the app needs to write captured content into your photo library workflows

The app currently does not rely on microphone data for its main vault features.

5. Advertising

CalculatorVault includes Google Mobile Ads features such as banner, interstitial, native, and app open ads. Ad providers may collect device or usage data according to their own privacy policies to deliver and measure ads.

If ads are disabled inside the app, related ad requests may stop for that app experience.

6. Browser and Third-Party Content

The app includes a browser view that can load external websites. When you visit third-party websites, those websites may collect data under their own policies. CalculatorVault does not control third-party website practices.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your vault media. Your vault media is primarily stored locally on your device and, if sync is enabled and available, may also be stored in your private CloudKit database through Apple.

8. Data Security

We use the iOS Keychain to store the generated vault user identifier and rely on Apple's platform protections, your device security settings, and your private iCloud account protections where CloudKit sync is used.

9. Children's Privacy

CalculatorVault is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us so we can review the issue.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
