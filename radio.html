Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 17, 2026

Overview

WorldRadio is a radio streaming app built for personal listening. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Data We Do Not Collect

WorldRadio does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Listening history sent to any external server
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to third parties

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

WorldRadio stores the following data only on your device using Apple's SwiftData and AppStorage frameworks:

  • Favorite Stations – radio stations you have marked as favorites
  • Recently Played Stations – a local history of stations you have listened to
  • Audio Quality Preference – your selected streaming quality setting

This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party. You can delete this data at any time from Settings → Storage within the app.

Network Requests

WorldRadio connects to the internet solely to stream audio from radio station URLs and to fetch station metadata (such as names, logos, and stream links) from a public radio directory. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

Third-Party Services

WorldRadio may use the Radio Browser API, a free and open community-driven radio station directory. Please refer to their own privacy policy for details on how they handle requests made to their service.

Children's Privacy

WorldRadio does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected at all, the app is safe for general use.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
