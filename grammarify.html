Grammarify - AI Keyboard

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

Welcome to Grammarify - AI Keyboard. Grammarify is built to help you improve grammar, rewrite text, change tone, translate content, and use AI-powered writing tools across apps. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Grammarify app and keyboard extension.

1. What Grammarify Collects

Grammarify collects only the information needed to provide account access, keyboard features, and AI writing assistance.

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and profile image if you sign in or create an account.
  • Keyboard request data such as the text you choose to process with AI features, selected tone, selected AI mode, and selected language.
  • App and keyboard preferences such as theme, appearance mode, selected app language, keyboard language choices, and custom AI tools you add or enable.

Grammarify does not request access to your photos, contacts, location, or advertising tracking in the app.

2. Account and Sign-In Information

Grammarify currently supports account access through email and password, Sign in with Apple, and Google Sign-In. If you use these features, we may receive information associated with your authentication method, such as your email address, display name, and profile image.

If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, your authentication data may be processed through trusted identity providers and authentication services such as Apple, Google Authentication.

Grammarify may also store a cached display name locally on your device to improve your in-app experience, especially when restoring Apple Sign-In profile details.

3. Keyboard Access and Full Access

Grammarify includes a custom iOS keyboard extension. To provide AI suggestions, network-based writing tools, and app-to-keyboard syncing, the keyboard may request Full Access in iOS.

  • Full Access allows the keyboard to connect to the internet for AI-powered suggestions.
  • Full Access also allows Grammarify to sync selected languages, themes, and custom tools between the app and keyboard.
  • When the keyboard is active, iOS may make text around the cursor available to the keyboard extension on your device.

Grammarify is designed so that text is sent for AI processing only when you actively use Grammarify's AI panels or related keyboard features.

4. AI Processing and Text You Submit

When you use features such as Fix Grammar, Change Tone, Ask AI, or Translate, the text you enter or keep in the active input context may be processed to generate suggestions.

Depending on the feature, this text may be sent securely to remote services or backend endpoints used by Grammarify to generate AI responses. This can include the text you are editing, selected prompt type, chosen tone, selected language, and related request context.

If you do not want text to be processed through AI features, do not activate Grammarify's AI tools for that content. Avoid using AI writing features for passwords, payment cards, government IDs, medical details, or other highly sensitive information.

5. Local Storage and On-Device Data

Grammarify stores certain information locally on your device and in the app group shared container used by the app and keyboard extension.

  • Appearance settings and selected theme
  • Selected app language and keyboard languages
  • Custom tone items, custom assistants, and ordering preferences
  • Locally cached AI catalog data used to keep the app and keyboard in sync
  • Session-related preferences needed to restore your experience

This local data is used to improve continuity between the main app and the keyboard extension.

6. How Grammarify Uses Information

Grammarify uses information only for legitimate app functionality, including:

  • Authenticating your account and restoring your session
  • Generating grammar, tone, AI, and translation suggestions
  • Saving your settings and syncing them between the app and keyboard
  • Improving reliability, preventing abuse, and troubleshooting errors
  • Allowing you to manage or delete your account from within the app

7. Third-Party Services

Grammarify may use trusted third-party services to support app functionality. These may include:

  • Google Sign-In for Google account authentication
  • Sign in with Apple for Apple account authentication
  • Remote AI or backend services used to process writing requests and return suggestions

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy terms to the extent necessary to provide their services to Grammarify.

8. Sharing, Sale, and Retention

Grammarify does not sell your personal information and is not designed to share your data for third-party advertising.

We may share information only when necessary to operate the service, authenticate users, process AI requests, comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the safety and security of users and the service.

We may retain account-related information while your account remains active, and we may retain limited technical logs or service records for security, rate limiting, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting. Data stored locally on your device may remain until you remove it, delete the app, disable the keyboard, or delete your account where applicable.

9. Your Choices

You can control your use of Grammarify in several ways:

  • Disable the keyboard extension in iOS Settings at any time
  • Turn off Full Access for the keyboard if you do not want network-enabled keyboard features
  • Choose not to use AI actions for specific text
  • Delete your account from within the app if that option is available in your account settings
  • Remove the app from your device to delete app data stored locally, subject to normal iOS behavior

10. Children's Privacy

Grammarify is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Grammarify, please contact us so we can review and address it.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements, service changes, operational requirements, or legal obligations. When we make updates, we will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date above.
